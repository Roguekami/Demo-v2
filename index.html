What the portal does

Every figure on this page comes from the demo school you can open above. Nothing here is a mockup of a feature that is not built.

Fee management

Set the termly amount per class, then track and collect fees effortlessly. Parents and students can pay securely directly through the portal, while schools can also record offline payments made via bank transfer, cash, or POS. Every student carries an amount due, an amount paid, and an automated running balance, making part payments simple to manage. Students and parents see their updated statement immediately from their own login.

Supports direct online payments through the portal as well as recording for offline bank transfers, cash, and POS.

Attendance

A teacher opens the class roster and every student starts marked present, so the register takes as long as the exceptions do. One save commits the whole day. Students and parents see the term summary as days present, days absent, and a percentage, not a raw log.

Results and report cards

Subject teachers enter a score and an optional remark for each student. Where a student sat the CBT exam, the score is already in the field. The portal totals the term, works out the average, and lays it out as a report card the school can print. Publishing results sends an SMS to the number on the student's record.

CBT exams

A teacher picks the subject and class, sets the window and the duration, and adds multiple-choice questions with the correct option marked. Publishing stays locked until at least one question exists. Students sit the exam on a screen of its own: a timer, one question at a time, a flag for anything to revisit, and a confirmation step so an exam cannot end by accident. Scores flow straight into results entry.

Also in every portal

Administrators post announcements and events to one feed that teachers and students both read. Teachers upload notes and past papers per class and subject, and students open them from their own Resources page. Classes and subjects are managed as a pair of linked panels, with a teacher assigned to each subject. A teacher who leaves is deactivated rather than deleted, so last year's results keep their author.

Parents do not get a separate account. They sign in with their child's login, which is how a parent sees fees, attendance, and results without the school maintaining a second set of credentials.
