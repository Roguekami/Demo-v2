Up and running in three steps

  1. 1

    Tell us about your school

    We collect your school name, structure (classes, arms, subjects), and staff list. Your branded portal is provisioned at yourschool.alimo.cloud within one business day.

  2. 2

    Import your existing data

    Upload student records as a spreadsheet. Our onboarding team reviews and imports the data for you, so your staff don't face a blank system on day one.

  3. 3

    Train your admin and go live

    A one-hour walkthrough covers daily workflows for your registrar, bursary, and teachers. Most schools are fully operational within a week of their first demo.
